Notes:

- This supersedes the earlier OOD table, which was computed on the non-canonical
  batch-size-256 run `e0_mobilenetv3_large_gpu_bs256_unfreeze2_amp`.
- The "Clean" row is recomputed by the OOD harness in full FP32 and differs from
  the canonical E0 test accuracy (0.982140) by roughly one sample (~2e-4), because
  the canonical training/eval used TF32. Drops are reported relative to this FP32
  clean baseline so all conditions share one pipeline.
- Robustness pattern: brightness/contrast shifts are tolerated well (<0.6 pt drop);
  Gaussian noise is the hardest corruption (accuracy 0.72, ECE rises to 0.12),
  followed by blur and the combined corruption. Severe-stage (Stage 3) recall is
  the most fragile under noise.
